# Fees and Placement Information (SAMPLE – replace with official documents)

## Fee payment
Tuition fees are paid semester-wise through the online payment link on the student portal. A late fee is charged if fees are not paid by the due date announced by the accounts office. Fee receipts can be downloaded from the portal.

## Scholarships
Eligible students can apply for government scholarships through the state scholarship portal. The college scholarship cell helps with document verification.

## Training and placements
The Training and Placement Cell conducts aptitude training, mock interviews and campus drives for final-year students. Students must register on the placement portal and maintain no active backlogs to be eligible for campus drives. Internship opportunities are shared through the placement portal.
