# Attendance and Examination Rules (SAMPLE – replace with official documents)

## Attendance
Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a valid medical certificate, subject to approval by the Head of Department.

## Examinations
Each semester has two internal sessional tests and one end-semester examination. Internal assessment carries 30% weightage and the end-semester exam carries 70%. A student must score at least 40% overall in a subject to pass. Students who fail may appear in the back-paper examination announced after results.

## Re-evaluation
Applications for re-evaluation must be submitted to the examination cell within 7 days of result declaration along with the prescribed fee.
