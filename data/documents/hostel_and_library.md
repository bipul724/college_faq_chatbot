# Hostel and Library Information (SAMPLE – replace with official documents)

## Hostel
Hostel rooms are allotted on a first-come, first-served basis after admission. To apply, fill the hostel application form from the student portal and submit it to the hostel warden's office with a copy of the admission receipt. Separate hostels are provided for boys and girls. Hostel gates close at 10:00 PM.

## Library
The central library is open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Each student can borrow up to 3 books for 14 days. A late fine of 2 rupees per day per book is charged after the due date. A valid college ID card is required to borrow books. Digital resources can be accessed from the library portal.
